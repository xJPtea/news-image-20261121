# Sources — The World Today, 21 November 2026

Every story on the front page was verified against a reputable source
before it was drawn. The rule applied: a story counts for 21 November
when it happened on that day or was first reported on 21 November 2026
in some time zone (several Friday-evening stories in Europe and the US
reached Asian readers on Saturday morning).

## The seven stories

1. **Middle East — Missiles over Riyadh.**
   Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted and destroyed three Houthi
   ballistic missiles fired toward Riyadh early on Saturday; civil
   defence alerts sounded at 01:53 and two explosions were heard.
   Source: Reuters wire, 21 Nov 2026 —
   https://kwsn.com/2026/11/20/saudi-arabia-issues-two-riyadh-alerts-says-danger-has-passed/
   and https://wireminute.com/s/saudi-intercepts-three-houthi-missiles-riyadh-third-night.html

2. **France — Three officers jailed for racist beating.**
   A Paris court sentenced three police officers over the 2020 beating
   of music producer Michel Zecler, upholding racism as an aggravating
   factor; firm prison terms of one to two years.
   Source: Le Monde, 21 Nov 2026 —
   https://www.lemonde.fr/en/france/article/2026/11/21/police-beating-of-black-music-producer-court-recognizes-racist-nature-of-violence-sentences-officers-to-prison-raising-hopes-for-new-legal-precedent_6785803_7.html

3. **Climate — COP31 talks deadlocked.**
   The COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, slipped into
   overtime as nearly 200 countries stayed split over fossil fuels and
   climate finance; the draft deal dropped fossil-fuel references.
   Source: Reuters, 20 Nov 2026 (talks ongoing into Saturday 21 Nov) —
   https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/cop31-climate-talks-spill-into-overtime-nations-quarrel-over-fossil-fuels-2026-11-20/

4. **Diplomacy — Trump: Iran wants a deal.**
   President Trump said at a White House press conference that Iran
   wants a deal but not one acceptable to him — "a real deal, not a
   fake deal" — while Iran's foreign minister said diplomacy could
   still end the war.
   Source: Reuters, 20 Nov 2026 (reported 21 Nov in Asia) —
   https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/araqchi-says-iran-wants-diplomacy-end-war-trump-says-terms-unacceptable-2026-11-20/
   and AP —
   https://www.ottumwacourier.com/news/national_news/trump-insists-iran-wont-make-an-acceptable-deal-as-concerns-grow-about-next-steps/article_79614b27-266e-5301-acbe-6ad5ca80c266.html

5. **Markets — Wall Street in correction.**
   US stocks fell sharply on Friday as AI and chip shares sold off;
   the S&P 500 fell about 3.7%, pushing it and the Dow into
   correction territory.
   Source: market reports, 21 Nov 2026 —
   https://jabulaniradio.com/rss/top-international-news-17
   and https://oneturn.news/today/

6. **California — Palisades fire arsonist convicted.**
   A federal jury convicted Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30, of two arson
   counts over the fire he lit on 1 January 2025 that became the
   Palisades Fire, which killed 12 people and destroyed about 6,800
   structures.
   Source: Reuters, 20 Nov 2026 (verdict Friday, reported 21 Nov in Asia) —
   https://www.reuters.com/world/suspect-los-angeles-deadly-palisades-fire-convicted-arson-retrial-2026-11-20/
   and CNN —
   https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/20/us/california-palisades-fire-trial-verdict?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta

7. **Europe — Young Europe marches.**
   Young protesters rallied across Europe on Friday over high living
   costs, housing, military spending and the climate crisis.
   Source: AFP, 21 Nov 2026 —
   https://www.nampa.org/text/23071857
